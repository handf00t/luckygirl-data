Support

Email handfoot119@gmail.com and say what happened. Your phone model and iOS version help.

Today's ranking will not load

The app needs one download a day. On a bad connection it falls back to the last day it managed to fetch and says so. Pull down on the ranking to retry.

The widget says "Today's up. Come look."

That is deliberate. The widget shows your lucky item only after you have opened your own card in the app — otherwise it would give away the day before you went looking for it.

Sharing to Instagram does nothing

Instagram must be installed; the share button is hidden if it is not. If it opens and refuses the story, make sure Instagram is up to date.

I picked the wrong animal

Tap Change animal at the top of the ranking. If you were born in January or early February you likely belong to the previous year's animal — the zodiac year turns at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January.

No morning reminder

Check Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ luckygirl. The reminder is scheduled on your device, so it also needs the app to have been opened at least once.
