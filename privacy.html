Privacy

luckygirl · last updated 16 August 2026

luckygirl collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you enter in the app is sent anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app:

  • the animal you picked and the year you picked it from
  • whether you have finished the intro
  • whether you have been asked about notifications
  • the last date you opened the ranking, and the last date you opened your own card
  • a copy of the most recent day's content, so the app still works offline

None of it is transmitted, backed up to us, or shared. We could not see it if we wanted to — there is nowhere for it to go.

What the app sends

One kind of request: an ordinary download of a public file that is identical for every reader, from

https://handf00t.github.io/luckygirl-data/

It carries no account, no identifier and no information about you. As with any web request, the host — GitHub, which serves the file — receives your IP address and stores it in its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs.

Notifications

The morning reminder is scheduled by your phone, on your phone. There is no push server and no device token, so nothing leaves the device and we have no way to tell whether you have notifications on.

Photos

If you choose one of your own photos as a story background, the app reads that single photo to draw it. It is never uploaded and never stored by us. It leaves your device only if you then post the story yourself.

Sharing to Instagram

Sharing hands the finished image to Instagram on your device and opens the app. From that point Instagram's own privacy policy applies. We are not told whether you posted it.

Children

luckygirl is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data — under GDPR, CCPA or anywhere else — apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none. Deleting the app removes everything it ever stored.

Changes

If this ever stops being accurate, this page changes before the app does, and the date at the top changes with it.
